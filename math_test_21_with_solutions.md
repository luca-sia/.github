## Question 1
What is 47 + 35?

**Working:**
```
  47
+ 35
----
  82
```
Step by step:
- 7 + 5 = 12 (write down 2, carry 1)
- 4 + 3 + 1 = 8

**Answer: 82**

## Question 2
A bakery has 84 cupcakes. They sell 38 cupcakes. How many cupcakes are left?

**Working:**
This is a subtraction problem.
```
  84
- 38
----
  46
```
Step by step:
- 4 - 8: We can't do this, so borrow from the 8
- 14 - 8 = 6
- 7 - 3 = 4 (remember we borrowed 1 from 8, so it became 7)

**Answer: 46 cupcakes**

## Question 4
72 stickers are shared equally among 9 children. How many stickers does each child get?

**Working:**
This is a division problem: 72 ÷ 9
Think: What number times 9 equals 72?
9 × 8 = 72
So 72 ÷ 9 = 8

**Answer: 8 stickers each**

## Question 5
What comes next in this pattern: 6, 12, 18, 24, ?

**Working:**
Look at the differences between numbers:
- 12 - 6 = 6
- 18 - 12 = 6  
- 24 - 18 = 6
The pattern adds 6 each time.
So next number: 24 + 6 = 30

**Answer: 30**

## Question 16
I think of a number. When I add 25 to it, I get 68. What number did I think of?

**Working:**
Let's call the mystery number ?
? + 25 = 68
To find ?, we subtract 25 from both sides:
? = 68 - 25 = 43
Check: 43 + 25 = 68 ✓

**Answer: 43**

## Question 20
What is the area of a rectangle that is 9cm long and 4cm wide?

**Working:**
Area of rectangle = length × width
Area = 9cm × 4cm = 36cm²
(Remember to write cm² for area)

**Answer: 36cm²**

## Tips for Problem Solving:

1. **Read the question carefully** - understand what it's asking
2. **Identify the operation** - addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division
3. **Show your working** - write down the steps
4. **Check your answer** - does it make sense?
5. **Look for patterns** - especially in number sequences
6. **Break down word problems** - what information do you have and what do you need to find?
7. **Remember your units** - cm, cm², minutes, pounds, etc.

**Practice these types of problems regularly, and you'll get better at solving them!**
